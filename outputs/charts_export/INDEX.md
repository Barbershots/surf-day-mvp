# Charts and maps — index

Every figure produced for the Brockenhurst aircraft-noise work. All are built
from the aircraft's own broadcast GPS data (ADS-B via OPDI / OpenSky, 2023–2025)
unless noted. "Measured" counts are minimums (a height can be pinned for about
1 in 5 arrivals).

## Route maps
- **brockenhurst_paths.png** — 3-year arrivals "rope" map: every airliner's path, fanning in from NE/E/S and converging over the village.
- **brockenhurst_paths_village.png** — the same, zoomed to street level over Brockenhurst.
- **brockenhurst_departures.png** — 3-year departures rope map (28,319 flights): the westerly fan plus the easterly runway-08 corridor over the village.
- **route_map.png** / **light_vs_airliner_map.png** — earlier route/track maps (airliners vs light aircraft).
- **origins_map.png** — Europe map of where arrivals depart from (departure airports).
- **ne_alignment_cross_vs_brockenhurst.png** — the CAA NE design envelope, georeferenced, showing the alignment cross at ~7 NM vs the village at 9.7 NM.
- **brockenhurst_join_direction.png** — where arrivals join the final approach line.
